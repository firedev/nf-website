Friends I love to train with.

Phuket

Benoit Piguet

@0xyben·Nama Freediving

  • Molchanovs Wave 3 Instructor Trainer
  • Certified Yoga Instructor

Benoit travelled the world for twelve years. Freediving, which he found on a trip to Thailand in 2019, is what made him settle down.

He practises breath-hold and yoga together, and each one feeds the other.

On his days off he trains static, his favourite discipline.

Court Burgon

@burgon_underwater·Nama Freediving

  • Molchanovs Wave 2 Instructor

Court studied zoology and moved to Phuket in 2017 to work in the water. More than a thousand hours as a scuba and freediving instructor have taught Court the local reef inside out.

Court teaches freediving, scuba and swimming to adults and kids, in relaxed and safe sessions.

Victoria Fawcus-Robinson

@barefootblondiephuket·ScubaNicks

  • SSI Senior Instructor
  • Freediving Instructor
  • Mermaid Instructor
  • SSI Scuba Explorer Instructor (kids)

Victoria started diving in 2015 and went pro in 2018. She is now Senior Instructor at ScubaNicks in Rawai, teaching every level up to dive professional.

She switches easily between freediving, scuba and mermaiding. She is patient with kids and first-timers and stays right beside them in the water.

Tony Myshlyaev

@tones.of.blue·Nama Freediving

  • Molchanovs Wave 3 Instructor
  • Underwater photographer

Thirteen years underwater, camera in hand. Tony taught freediving on Koh Tao for seven years before moving to Phuket in 2022.

His photographer’s eye shows in his coaching: body position, movement, wasted effort. He works with divers of every level.

Bali

Yan Tychkovskyi

Bali Freedivers·@balifreedivers.amed·Amed

  • SSI Level 3
  • Molchanovs

Yan is Ukrainian and has been in the water since he was five. He moved to Indonesia in 2013 and has since taught more than five hundred students across five countries.

He runs Bali Freedivers in Amed and works mostly with beginners. He shapes each course around what you do in the water, spearfishing or surfing, and teaches the breathing and safety skills that hold up when things get tight.

Moscow

Roman Garaikhanov

@roman_gara·Molchanova Freediving School

  • Molchanovs Wave 3 Instructor
  • Molchanovs Kids Instructor L1 & L2

Roman grew up in the water and swam competitively for his city team before freediving took over.

He still competes and keeps refining his own diving while he coaches. He is certified to teach adults (Wave 3) and kids (L1 & L2) in the Molchanovs system, and he is an experienced safety diver.

He organises the amateur I Love Freediving competitions and is sports manager at the Natalia Molchanova Freediving School.

Artur Tikhonov

@artur_tichonov·Molchanova Freediving School

  • Molchanovs Wave 2 Instructor
  • Freediving rescue & first aid

Artur is a competitive finswimmer turned freediving instructor, with national titles in both bi-fins and monofin.

Personal bests: 248 m bi-fins, 227 m monofin, 161 m breaststroke and a 7:24 static. He teaches Wave 1 and Wave 2 in the pool and is a certified freediving rescue and first-aid specialist.
