Freediving gives your life back.

The whole underwater world is in the first ten metres.

Below that there is nothing left to look at, so you look inward. We go out to sea and drift over open water. The states you reach down there go very deep.

Everything turns up at once.

Hold your breath, drop below the surface, and every thought you own arrives together. You start filtering. The noise drops.

Then there is room inside you.

Your head fights it first. On the way down you hear it — get out, we are drowning. Calm it and it goes quiet. It is still quiet when you surface, and it stays quiet at the desk, in the hard conversation, at 3am.

Deeper means calmer.

Equalize early, turn when you planned to, and come up with air to spare.
