Why freediving

Deeper means calmer. You go down relaxed or you do not go down. Equalize early, turn when you planned to, and come up with air to spare.

Water teaches calm for life. Children learn to float, swim, glide and dive with an instructor beside them, one skill at a time.

Move with your whole body.

Bi-fins, monofin, no fins, or hands on the line — relaxed movement is the same skill in every one of them. The line, the lanyard and your buddy keep it safe while you learn.

One diver. One buoy.

You get the whole session. No group to keep up with, no waiting your turn on the line.
