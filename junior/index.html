Calm starts young.

Children learn to float, swim, glide and dive with an instructor beside them, one skill at a time.

Twenty metres in one session.

For two years Eva turned at twelve metres, sure she could not go deeper. One session on the line with me, and she fell straight to twenty.

“First I was scared that I was falling. Then I suddenly relaxed, and music started playing in my head.”

Eva, 13

The dive reflex starts in the pool.

Hold your breath with your face in the water and your heart slows down by itself. The same response that keeps a diver calm at depth settles a child before a test at school.

Posture and lung capacity.

Regular sessions straighten the back and train the lungs to take in more air.

Focus that holds.

Underwater a child has one job, and attention turns calm and sharp.

Swim and dive safely.

Kids learn to swim on the surface, dive under and come back up safely.

A love of the water.

The sea is alive. Kids learn to care for it and leave it as they found it.

One instructor, one child.

Your child gets my full attention and sets the pace. Pool first. Open water once the basics are calm and automatic. We take as long as it takes.

Progress you can see.

Kids set small goals and hit them. A longer float. A cleaner duck dive. A quiet recovery after a hold.

Parents stay in the loop.

After each session I tell you what changed: comfort in the water, focus, recovery. You always know how your child is doing.
